What is scraping risk?

Web scraping is automated extraction of data from pages or APIs.

Even when data is "public", abuse can still happen at scale: competitor intelligence, account profiling, pricing undercutting, and infrastructure load spikes.

How and why scraping abuse works
  1. Bot sends repeated requests to predictable endpoints.
  2. Responses are parsed into structured records.
  3. Process repeats continuously with low per-request cost.
  4. Without controls, extraction scales faster than manual browsing.

Why: machine-to-machine traffic can be cheap and parallel, while your server still pays CPU, bandwidth, and data exposure cost.
